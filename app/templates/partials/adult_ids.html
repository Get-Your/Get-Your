Accepted Adult Identification

Federal Government Identification

  • U.S. Passport

  • U.S. Passport Card

  • Permanent Resident Card (Green Card) - Form I-551

  • Employment Authorization Document (EAD) - Form I-766

  • U.S. Military Identification Card

  • Military Dependent Identification Card

State and Local Government Identification

  • State Driver's License (issued by any U.S. state or territory)

  • State Identification Card (Non-Driver ID)

  • Government-Issued Employee Identification Card (Federal, State, County, or Municipal)

  • Tribal Identification Card (issued by a federally recognized tribal government)

Other Government-Issued Identification

  • U.S. Coast Guard Merchant Mariner Credential/Card

  • Department of Veterans Affairs Veteran Health Identification Card (VHIC)

  • Foreign Government-Issued National Identification Card

  • Foreign Passport

  • Canadian Provincial Driver's License
